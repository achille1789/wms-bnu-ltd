# WMS BNU App

The **WMS BNU App** is a warehouse management solution designed to support the provisioning of industrial equipment and supplies.  
It serves as a central provider for managing inventory, streamlining supply workflows, and ensuring accurate tracking of goods.

## Purpose

- Provide a reliable platform for managing industrial equipment and supplies.
- Enable efficient warehouse operations through clear inventory control.
- Support scalability and adaptability for industrial environments.

## Features (Planned/Initial)

- Centralised repository of equipment and supply data.
- Inventory tracking and stock management.
- Simple, intuitive interface for warehouse operators.
- Integration-ready design for future expansion.

## Repository Structure

This repository will contain:
- Source code for the WMS BNU App.
- Documentation related to setup, usage, and contribution.
- Future integrations and modules as the application evolves.
